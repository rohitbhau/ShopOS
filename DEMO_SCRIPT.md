# ShopOS - Demo Video Script (5 Minutes)

Complete script for recording a 5-minute product demo video.

---

## Video Structure

**Total Duration**: 5:00 minutes  
**Target Audience**: Small shop owners in India  
**Language**: Hindi + English subtitles  
**Device**: Mid-range Android phone (Redmi Note / Samsung M series)  
**Setting**: Inside an actual kirana store

---

## Script

### [0:00 - 0:30] Opening Hook

**Visuals**: Shop owner struggling with physical register, messy papers, calculator  
**Voiceover (Hindi)**:
> "Har din kitne invoice? Kitna maal becha? Kaun customer ne udhar liya? Yaad rakhna mushkil hai na?"  
> (Translation: How many invoices daily? How much sold? Which customer took credit? Hard to remember, right?)

**On Screen Text**: "90% छोटी दुकानों को stock और account tracking में problem आती है"  
(Translation: 90% small shops face problems in stock and account tracking)

**Transition**: Phone screen lights up, ShopOS app logo appears

---

## Recording Notes

### Camera Setup
- **Device**: 2 phones - 1 for demo, 1 for recording
- **Resolution**: 1080p minimum
- **Frame Rate**: 30 fps
- **Orientation**: Portrait (9:16 for social media)

### Lighting
- Natural daylight in store
- Ring light for closeups
- No harsh shadows on phone screen

### Audio
- Lavalier mic for voiceover
- Record in quiet room (record VO separately, overlay later)
- Background: Soft music (royalty-free)

### Editing
- Software: CapCut / Premiere Rush
- Transitions: Quick cuts (0.5s max)
- Text: Large, readable font (Noto Sans)
- Subtitles: Hindi + English (burnt in)
- Logo: Bottom-right corner throughout

### Versions to Create

1. **Primary** (5:00): Full version for YouTube, website
2. **Short** (1:00): Key highlights for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
3. **WhatsApp** (0:30): Ultra-short teaser with download link
4. **Regional** (5:00 each): Tamil, Telugu, Marathi voiceovers
